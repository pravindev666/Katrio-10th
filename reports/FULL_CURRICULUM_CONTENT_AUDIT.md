## 3. Subject-Level Prerequisites, Common Student Confusions & Learning Outcomes

### A. Mathematics (Chapters 1–8, 150 Subsections)
- **Prerequisites:** Class 8–9 sets, polynomials, linear equations, triangle congruence, Pythagoras basics, coordinate plotting, trigonometric ratios, mensuration of basic solids, and mean/median/mode.
- **Top Student Confusions:** Confusing a relation with a function; assuming `f∘g = g∘f`; assuming matrix multiplication is commutative (`AB ≠ BA`); dropping excluded values before cancelling rational factors; confusing Thales' BPT with Angle Bisector Theorem; mixing up Variance (`σ²`) vs Standard Deviation (`σ`) vs Coefficient of Variation (`CV`); swapping elevation/depression horizontal line of sight.
- **What Must Be Visualized & Calculated:** Dynamic labelled 2D proofs (`A, B, C, D, E`), live ratio/slope/determinant invariants, interactive 2×2 matrix row×column dot-products, live data dot-plots for `σ`, and 3D+2D net pairings for Ch. 7 solids.

### B. Science — Physics (Units 1–6, 117 Subsections)
- **Prerequisites:** Class 9 motion graphs, force/mass basics, reflection/refraction intro, heat & temperature, sound wave basics, Ohm's law intro, atomic structure.
- **Top Student Confusions:** Weightlessness in orbit mistaken for "zero gravity" (really continuous free-fall `a = g`); confusing Electric Potential (`V = W/Q`) with Current (`I`); assuming heat `H` scales linearly with `I` instead of quadratically (`H = I²Rt`); reversing Myopia (concave) vs Hypermetropia (convex); assuming real liquid expansion equals apparent expansion; confusing nuclear fission vs fusion vs α/β/γ decay displacement laws.
- **What Must Be Visualized:** Interactive 2D ray/field/graph labs with real SI units, locked-variable comparison missions, and verifiable textbook numericals (e.g., Solved Problem 2 `V=100/10=10 V`, Solved Problem 6 `H=54,000 J`).

### C. Science — Chemistry (Units 7–11, 75 Subsections)
- **Prerequisites:** Valency, chemical symbols, balancing simple equations, atomic number/mass number, acids/bases intro.
- **Top Student Confusions:** Calling undissolved solute sitting at the bottom of a beaker "supersaturated" (it is **saturated + undissolved excess**; true supersaturation requires hot dissolution + calm cooling); forgetting why Cryolite & Fluorspar are added in Hall's Al process; skipping atom conservation in double displacement; confusing IUPAC prefix/root/suffix order; forgetting hard-water Ca²⁺/Mg²⁺ scum formation with soaps vs detergents.
- **What Must Be Visualized:** **Bright interactive 2D chemical process flowcharts with sliders** (`Reactants → Conditions → Molecular/Tank Change → Products → Observations → Balanced Equation`) paired with 3D molecular geometry ONLY where spatial bonding clarifies structure (Units 7 & 11).

### D. Science — Biology & Visual Communication (Units 12–23, 179 Subsections)
- **Prerequisites:** Plant/animal cells, organ systems overview, pollination basics, DNA/chromosomes intro, ecosystem conservation.
- **Top Student Confusions:** Mixing plant hormones (Auxin/Cytokinin/Gibberellin/ABA/Ethylene) with human endocrine glands; confusing leech nephridia (17 pairs) with mammalian kidneys; assuming mothers determine a child's sex instead of the father's `X`/`Y` sperm (`44+XX` vs `44+XY`); confusing homologous (divergent) vs analogous (convergent) organs; HIV transmission myths; mixing dicot vs monocot vascular bundle arrangements.
- **What Must Be Visualized:** 3D+2D synchronized anatomy ONLY for Heart, Brain/Neuron, and Root/Stem/Leaf blocks; **calm, clinical, textbook-faithful 2D diagrams** for Reproduction, Hormones, Leech/Rabbit systems, Genetics, Biotechnology rDNA cut-and-splice, and Environmental Management.

### E. English (Units 1–7, 21 Selections — Zero 3D)
- **Exact Structure:** Each unit weaves 1 Prose, 1 Poem (4 marked `*` Memoriter), 1 Supplementary story, plus integrated Listening, Speaking, Reading, Writing, Vocabulary, and Grammar exercises.
- **Top Student Confusions:** Memorizing generic summaries without understanding character motivation, confusing poetic devices (Metaphor vs Simile vs Personification vs Alliteration vs Transferred Epithet), active/passive voice & direct/indirect speech transformations.
- **Licensing Flag:** Modern selections (*Zigzag*, *The Attic*, *I am Every Woman*, *The House on Elm Street*, *The Night the Ghost Got In*, *His First Flight*) carry third-party author copyrights in addition to `© SCERT 2019`. Never copy full prose/poems verbatim; use original paraphrased storyboards, glossary cues, and page-anchored citations.

### F. Tamil (7 இயல்கள், 36 Sections — Zero 3D)
- **Exact Structure (Verified 2025 Edition):** 7 thematic இயல்கள் integrating உரைநடை (Prose), செய்யுள் (Poetry — 9 marked `*` மனப்பாடப் பகுதிகள் including திருக்குறள்), துணைப்பாடம் (Supplementary), and progressive இலக்கணம் (`சொல்`, `தொகாநிலைத் தொடர்கள்`, `தொகைநிலைத் தொடர்கள்`, `இலக்கணம் – பொது`, `அகப்பொருள்`, `புறப்பொருள்`, `பா வகை & அலகிடுதல்`).
- **Top Student Confusions:** Distinguishing தொகைநிலை vs தொகாநிலைத் தொடர்கள்; classifying ஐம்பால், வழு vs வழாநிலை vs 5 வழுவமைதி; separating அகப்பொருள் (ஐந்திணை முதல்/கரு/உரிப்பொருள்) vs புறப்பொருள் (12 திணைகள்); scanning நேர்/நிரை அசை in அலகிடுதல் (இயல் 7).
- **Educator Review Requirement:** All Tamil literary & grammar explanations must undergo formal review by a qualified Tamil-language educator before being marked `editorial_status: published`.

### G. Social Science (27 Units: 10 History, 7 Geography, 5 Civics, 5 Economics, 116 Subsections)
- **Exact Structure:** World + Indian + Tamil Nadu History (pp. 1–128), India + Tamil Nadu Physical/Human Geography (pp. 129–238), Indian Constitution/Central/State/Foreign Policy Civics (pp. 239–291), GDP/Globalization/Food Security/Taxes/TN Clusters Economics (pp. 292–338).
- **Top Student Confusions:** Mixing WWI vs WWII alliances and dates; confusing SW Monsoon (Jun–Sep) vs NE Retreating Monsoon (Oct–Dec, Tamil Nadu's main rainy season); confusing Union vs State executive powers; confusing progressive Direct Taxes vs shiftable Indirect GST; separating documented textbook historical interpretations from present-day facts.
- **What Must Be Visualized:** **Accurate real-coastline 2D satellite maps** (World, India, Tamil Nadu) with subsection-specific GPS pins, cause-effect timelines, constitutional flowcharts, and transparent economic calculators.
