## 2. Transparent Rule A7 Historical & Current-World Accuracy Notes

Per **Rule A7** (never silently rewrite the textbook, and never present an outdated or disputed textbook print date without transparent context), **three factual discrepancies** identified directly in `Class_10_Social_Science_English_2025_Edition` (pp. 40–52) are explicitly surfaced via dedicated `updatedContextNote` callouts inside the affected lessons:

| Subsection | Printed Page (PDF Page) | What the 2025 TNSCERT Textbook Prints | Transparent Updated / Historiographical Context Note Surfaced in Katrio |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **4.1 Chinese Revolution** (`4.1(a)`) | p. 41 (PDF p. 47) | Prints Opium Wars as *"1832 and 1848"* | Explains that international historical records date the First Opium War to **1839–1842** (Treaty of Nanking, 1842) and the Second Opium War to **1856–1860**; advises students to note the textbook wording for board recall while understanding the standard historical dates. |
| **4.7 Arab-Israeli War** | p. 45 (PDF p. 51) | Prints separate Armistice Agreements between Israel and Arab neighbours as *"February and June 1947"* | Clarifies that because Israel declared statehood on **14 May 1948**, the UN-mediated Armistice Agreements took place in **February–July 1949** (Rhodes Armistice Agreements); notes the likely printing slip without erasing the textbook reference. |
| **4.9 Towards European Union** | p. 48 (PDF p. 54) | Prints *"Today the European Union has 28 member states"* | Clarifies that following the United Kingdom's departure (**Brexit**, 31 January 2020), the European Union currently has **27 member states**; guides students clearly on both the 2025 textbook sentence and current-world reality. |

## 3. Fact vs. Interpretation & Age-Appropriate Handling of War History

- **Zero gamification of war casualties:** None of the ten History Unit 4 interactives (`4.1`–`4.10`) use combat scoreboards, attack/kill buttons, countdown stress timers, or war-game mechanics. All 10 bespoke 2D explorers focus on **diplomatic treaties, alliance maps, timelines, decolonization turning points, and peaceful institutional integration** (NAM, EU, German reunification).
- **Fact vs. Interpretation separation (Stage 4):** Every causal step explicitly separates documented historical events/treaties (`Evidence`) from analytical framing (`Interpretation` badge when summarizing competing Cold War ideological viewpoints such as US containment vs. Soviet security buffer).
- **Textbook Exercise vs. Original Practice separation (Stage 7):** Every exam-bridge prompt carries an explicit origin badge (`Textbook Exercise Reference` citing exact pp. 50–52 question types vs. `Original Practice` authored specifically for conceptual understanding).
